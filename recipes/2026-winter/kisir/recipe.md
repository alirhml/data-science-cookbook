# Kısır (Turkish Bulgur Salad)

Kısır is a traditional Turkish salad made with fine bulgur,
fresh vegetables, herbs, olive oil, and lemon juice.

It is a popular dish in Turkish cuisine, especially at family
gatherings. I chose this recipe because it is simple to prepare
and represents traditional Turkish home cooking.

**Provided by:** Sefa

## Stats

- Cooking Time: 30 minutes
- Servings: 4

- 200 g fine bulgur
- 250 ml hot water
- 1 tablespoon tomato paste

- 3 spring onions
- 1 medium tomato
- 1 cucumber
- 1 handful fresh parsley
- 1 teaspoon salt
- 1/2 teaspoon ground cumin

# Vegan Scrambled Eggs
This is a recipe for vegan scrambled eggs. It's made of tofu and for this delicious meal no animals are harmed or exploited. 

![Vegan scrambled eggs with bread and fruits from my birthday last year](image.jpg)

**Provided by:** Rikki 

## Stats
- Cooking Time: 15-20 minutes
- Servings: 2 (when served as a side)

## Ingredients
- 1 block of plain tofu (appox. 200g)
- Vegetables of your choice (I like using tomatoes and onions)
- 1 tbsp oil for frying
- 2 tbsp soy yoghurt 
- 1 tsp agave syrup
- 1 tsp sweet paprika
- ½ tsp turmeric (for the colour)
- ½ tsp kala namak (Indian black salt)
- A handful of chives

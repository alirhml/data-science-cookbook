# Vegetarian Lasagna
This dish is a vegetarian option for the classic lasagna which we all love. It has lots of flavors and is easy to make. Give it a try!

![lasagna_photo](image.jpg)

**Provided by:** Michl

## Stats
- Overall Cooking Time: 1h 10min
- Servings: 4

## Nutritional Information (per serving)
- Energy: 366 kcal
- Protein: 15.5 g
- Fat: 29.6 g
- Carbohydrates: 9.6 g

## Ingredients
For the sauce:
- 250 g zucchini
- 250 g tomatoes
- 100 g mushrooms
- 1 small onion
- 2 tbsp oil
- 1 tbsp flour
- 0.5 tsp vegetable bouillon powder
- 0.5 tsp oregano
- a pinch of salt
- 100 g heavy cream

Also:
- oil for the baking dish
- lasagna sheets (no pre-cooking required)
- 200 g Gouda (grated)

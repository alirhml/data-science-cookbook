# Mexican Molletes 
Molletes are a traditional Mexican dish. Normally eaten for breakfast or dinner. Original made from leftovers, today are fresh made. Several versions exist, with mushrooms, sausages or ham. Today we are making the basic version. It is made out of bread, beans, cheese and a sauce compose of fresh tomato, onion, jalapeño chili and coriander named *Pico de Gallo*

Out of the Jalapeños, which are not easy to find but easy to substitute for other chili, the ingredients are worldwide available. Being an easy homesick remedies. If you do not eat chili, you can skip it. The tomato, coriander and onion freshness will be enough.

## Stats
- Cooking Time: 30 min
- Servings: 6

## Ingredients
### Beans
- 1 Can of Beans (Kidney or Black) 
- 1/4 Medium Onion
- 1 Garlic Clove
- 20 to 50 ml Milk
- 2 Tbps Butter

### Sauce
- 2 Tomatoes
- 1/2 Medium Onion 
- 5 g Coriander
- 0 to 2 Jalapeños, depends on how spicy the Chilies are and how much you eat.
- 1/2 Lime or Lemon, Lime is prefer. 
- Salt and Pepper to taste.

### Main Dish
- 3 Bread-rolls (Brötchen)
- 100 g Cheese, any melting cheese would make it, eg Gouda or Pizza mix. 

## Tools and Kitchen Aids
- Small Pot (Stainless Steel)
- Sieve
- Potato Masher or Blender
- Oven
- Bread Knife
- Cutting Board
- Bowl
- Metal Table Spoons

## Instructions
### Beans
- Step 1: Open the can and put the beans in the sieve. 
- Step 2: Rinse the beans under the faucet with cold water and let them to drain.
- Step 3: Cut the onion and garlic clove into small squares/pieces.
- Step 4: Put the pot in medium heat and wait until is warm, then add the butter; do not let it burn.
- Step 5: Once the butter is melt, add the onion. Wait until begins to get brown.  Then add the garlic.
- Step 6: Look into the garlic, once it is soft and begins to get brown add the beans and 20 ml of milk.
- Step 7: Smash the beans with the potato masher until all the beans are smashed. If the mixture is too dry, add more milk. It must not be a liquid, but a paste that you can steer.
- Step 8: ***Steer and Steer*** You need to continuously, not with effort, but continuously stir the mix for approx 5 min. Otherwise the beans will stick to the pot's bottom and will burn out. It is suggested to use a metal table spoon. After 5 minutes, turnoff the heat and let the beans rest. From now on, steer from time to time. Don't let the beans get stuck to the pot.
- Step 9: Preheat the oven to 175 °C

### Pico de Gallo
- Step 10: Prepare the sauce. Cut in small pieces the onion, the tomato and the Jalapeño or chili if you are putting some, and the coriander too. Put everything in the bowl. Add the juice of the lime, and salt and paper to taste. Finally mix everything.
### Main Dish 
- Step 11: Cut the bread into half and remove part of the internal bread crumbs. 
- Step 12: To each half, add between 2 and 3 tablespoons of beans. Spread them through the bread. 
- Step 13: Put the cheese on top of the bread.
- Step 14: Place the molletes on tray and bake them between 5 and 8 min. Take them out once the cheese has melted or begins to get brown.
- Step 15: Serve them in a plate and add the the pico de gallo just before you eat them.

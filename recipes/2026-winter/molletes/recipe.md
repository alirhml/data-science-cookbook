Molletes are a traditional brakfast or dinner mexican food. They are made from leftovers. Altough several versions exist, the basic one is made out of brad, beans, cheese and a souce compose of fresh tomato, onion, jalapeño chilli and corianda.

## Stats
- Cooking Time: 30 min
- Servings: 5

## Ingredients
### Beans
- 1 Can of Beans (Kidney or Black) 
- 1/4 Medium Onion
- 1 Garlic Clove
- 10 ml Milk
- 2 Tbps Butter

### Sauce
- 2 Tomatoes
- 1/2 Medium Onion 
- 5 g Corianda
- 0 to 2 Jalapeños, depends on how spicy it is and how much chilli you eat.
- 1/2 Lime or Lemon, Lime is prefeer. 
- Salt and Papper to taste.

### Main Dish
- 3 Breadrolls (Brötchen)
- 100 g Cheese, any melting cheese would make it.

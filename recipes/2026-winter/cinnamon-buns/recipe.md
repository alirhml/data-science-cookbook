# Cinnamon buns
This is a recipe for vegan cinnamon buns. I especially like to make them in autumn and winter and they remind me of Sweden.

![Cinnamon buns](image.jpg)

**Provided by:** Finja

## Stats
- Cooking Time: 1h 55min
- Servings: 12 cinnamon buns

## Ingredients
Yeast Dough

- 500 g flour
- 1 packet dry yeast or ½ cube fresh yeast
- 100 g sugar
- ½ tsp salt
- 50 g plant-based margarine
- 250 g plant-based milk = 250 ml (e.g. soy milk, oat milk…)

Cinnamon Filling

- 60 g plant-based margarine
- 60 g sugar
- 2 tsp cinnamon

Icing

- 100 g powdered sugar
- 1 tbsp plant-based milk

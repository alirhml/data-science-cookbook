# Apple Crumble
A warm, buttery crumble over soft cinnamon apples. Quick to make, with ingredients you usually have at home.

**Provided by:** Maike

## Stats
- Cooking Time: 15 minutes preparation, 35 minutes baking
- Servings: 4

## Ingredients
- 4 apples (e.g. Boskoop or Elstar)
- 1 tbsp lemon juice
- 1 tsp cinnamon
- 2 tbsp sugar (for the apples)
- 150 g flour
- 100 g cold butter, in cubes
- 80 g sugar (for the crumble)
- 1 pinch of salt

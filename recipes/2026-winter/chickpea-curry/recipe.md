# Chickpea Curry
A healthy Indian-inspired chickpea curry packed with vitamins and plant-based protein. Perfect for cold days!

[Plate of chickpea-curry](image.jpg)

**Provided by:** Finn

## Stats
- Cooking Time: 45 mins
- Servings: 4

## Ingredients
* 2 cans (400 g each) chickpeas, drained and rinsed
* 1 can (400 g) kidney beans, drained and rinsed
* 2 medium onions, finely chopped
* 2 medium tomatoes, chopped, or 1 can of chopped tomatoes
* 1 small brocoli
* 1 clove of garlic, finely chopped
* 150 g of spinach
* A small piece of fresh ginger (about 1 tsp), finely grated
* 2 tablespoons coconut oil
* 1 teaspoon cumin seeds
* 1 teaspoon ground coriander
* ½ teaspoon turmeric
* 1 teaspoon red chili powder, adjusted to taste
* 1 teaspoon garam masala
* Salt
* 250–350 ml water, as needed
* Fresh coriander leaves, for garnish (if it doesn´t taste like soup for you)
* Lemon juice, optional
